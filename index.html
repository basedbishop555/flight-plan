Before you sign anything

Flight Plan can sign for the locks and reward tokens your connected wallet owns. Exactly three kinds of signature, nothing else:

  1. A vote. One allocate call to the Aero Voter for a lock you own, 0 ETH. It moves votes, never tokens.
  2. A sweep approval. An exact-amount approve to Aero's Metarouter on a reward token the scan found in your wallet.
  3. A sweep swap. One Metarouter execute that sells those rewards for USDC, paid to your own wallet.

Never: an approval on sAERO, setApprovalForAll, a transfer, a claim, an unlimited approval, or any value other than 0 ETH. Every signature is rehearsed on the chain first (a dry run) and checked by the open-source guard before it reaches your wallet. Reject anything that looks different.

Check the address bar says flight-plan.app. A copy on another address can be anything.
