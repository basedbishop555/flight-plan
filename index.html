Before you sign anything

Flight Plan can sign for the locks and reward tokens your connected wallet owns. Exactly four kinds of signature, nothing else:

  1. An allocation. One allocate call to the Aero Voter on Base for a lock you own. It moves your allocation, never tokens. On Base it carries 0 ETH; when the split uses another network it carries that network's message fee (Aero's own quote + 10%, never over 0.03 ETH; the bridge refunds the unused part to you), and moving AERO between networks takes two of these.
  2. A sweep approval. An exact-amount approve to Aero's Metarouter on a reward token the scan found in your wallet.
  3. A sweep swap. One Metarouter execute that sells those rewards for USDC, paid to your own wallet.
  4. A gas bridge, only when you tap Get gas. A deposit of about $1 of ETH (Ethereum: $5, never over 0.006 ETH) to Relay's Base contract, delivered as gas to your own address on another network.

Never: an approval on sAERO, setApprovalForAll, a transfer, a claim, an unlimited approval, or any ETH value except an allocation's quoted network fee and that capped gas bridge. Every signature is rehearsed on the chain first (a dry run) and checked by the open-source guard before it reaches your wallet. Reject anything that looks different.
