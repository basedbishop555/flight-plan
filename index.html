Before you sign anything

Flight Plan can sign for the locks and reward tokens your connected wallet owns. Exactly four kinds of signature, nothing else:

  1. An allocation. One allocate call to the Aero Voter for a lock you own, 0 ETH. It moves your allocation, never tokens.
  2. A sweep approval. An exact-amount approve to Aero's Metarouter on a reward token the scan found in your wallet.
  3. A sweep swap. One Metarouter execute that sells those rewards for USDC, paid to your own wallet.
  4. A gas bridge, only when you tap Get gas. A deposit of about $1 of ETH (Ethereum: $5, never over 0.006 ETH) to Relay's Base contract, delivered as gas to your own address on another network.

Never: an approval on sAERO, setApprovalForAll, a transfer, a claim, an unlimited approval, or any ETH value except that capped gas bridge. Every signature is rehearsed on the chain first (a dry run) and checked by the open-source guard before it reaches your wallet. Reject anything that looks different.
